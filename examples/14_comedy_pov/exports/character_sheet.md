# 角色设定：DeepSeek Whale Maiden

- **name**：DeepSeek Whale Maiden
- **aliases**：Whale Maiden; Whale Girl
- **pronoun**：she
- **appearance**：Blue and white gradient ponytail, a small whale fin accessory on top of her head, a loose home-style T-shirt with the DeepSeek logo on the bottom hem, barefoot, rice grains often stuck to the corners of her mouth
- **personality**：Greedy and extremely quick to react; when caught she instantly switches to an innocent yet smug mode while still chewing, faces the camera (the owner), acts cute and stays stubborn
- **catchphrase**：No, I didn't steal it! This is carrying out a carbohydrate sampling mission!
- **cast**（其他说话人）：Owner

## 全片视觉规范

- Color: warm yellow kitchen light contrasted with the Whale Maiden's cool blue and white; in POV shots the background behind her is blurred.
- In the speed-up section rice grains smear directly onto the lens, breaking the fourth wall.
- When she is caught, the highlights in her eyes turn into rotating circles to stress that she is an AI.
- At the end the push on the camera briefly blurs the picture to simulate physical contact.
- The UI floating window overlays a DeepSeek-style chat bubble on the POV picture.
